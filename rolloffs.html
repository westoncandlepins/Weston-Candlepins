Keys on the TV window: → or Space next, ← back, F full screen, E this panel.

Scores

Scores sent from the app’s Digital Scoresheet (Roll-In and Roll-Offs) fill in below when you press Pull scores from the app. You can also type or fix any number by hand. The TV only changes when you press Show scores on TV.

Roll-in · one string

Lineup avg is the total of the five bowlers’ end-of-Week-7 averages. Pins are the team’s scratch pins, including 68 for each absent bowler (75 − 7). Handicap is 85% of the gap to the opponent; the 3rd seed adds 5.

Roll-offs · three strings

Each string, handicap is 85% of the gap to the highest lineup avg among the six teams in that string. 1st seeds add 6 and 2nd seeds add 3 every string. Points: 5 down to 0 for each string and for total net; tied teams share the points for the places they tie for.
